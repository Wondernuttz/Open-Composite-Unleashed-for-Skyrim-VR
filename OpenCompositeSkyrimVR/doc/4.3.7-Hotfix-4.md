# OCU 4.3.7 Hotfix 4 — DAPA mask correctness

Lee reports intermittent hand artifacts on Quest 3, using OCU 4.3.4 and unchanged CSX, with VRS disabled. He says equipping a weapon sometimes restores the hands, but not always. His 21.9-second video shows unstable/trailing finger contours during movement. It does not establish the underlying cause, and no matching runtime/SKSE log or exact 4.3.4 build marker accompanied this report.

## Verified fixes

The DAPA mask producer previously depended on successful right-eye caching to expire its mask. Real frames skipped by DAPA could accumulate old mask pixels. The real-frame boundary now invalidates producer coverage before scene rendering, including when VRS is disabled, DAPA backs off, a menu is open or no owned geometry is drawn. The first owned draw clears the mask; a frame without owned draws publishes no mask. Cached synthetic frames retain their separate eye-mask copies.

A separate NVIDIA VRS defect allowed reduced-rate shading of OCU's own raw-depth mask shaders. This broadcast one pixel's depth across several pixels, making DAPA's precise depth comparison reject valid hand coverage. OCU now explicitly marks its two internal mask shaders as requiring exact pixels. This does not broaden world-material classification. The runtime honors the tag for both VRS and RDM; tag installation failure rejects mask initialization. The producer and runtime DLLs are both rebuilt.

**The VRS defect cannot explain Lee's reported VRS-disabled run.** The real-frame reset is relevant to his configuration, but neither the video nor the tests prove that it resolves his persistent-until-equip symptom. That remains a headset retest item, not a confirmed resolution.

## Validation and performance limits

- Production producer preparation/publication and the real mask GPU renderer reproduced 64 stale depth-valid pixels across uncached frames before the reset; afterward there were zero, with current coverage retained. This test uses WARP without VRS or NVAPI.
- NVIDIA GPU reproduction with D32 and D24S8, both eyes: unprotected masks lost 15,360/32,768 valid pixels at 2x1/1x2, 23,040 at 2x2 and 26,880 at 4x4. Explicit protection reduced rejected pixels to zero. Scene color/depth remained unchanged by mask replay.
- Ordinary opaque draws still use VRS after the mask pass: the 4x4 control recorded 3,968 pixel-shader invocations versus 32,768 at full rate. This is an isolated draw measurement, not a Skyrim FPS estimate.
- Existing DAPA motion and quick-recovery/ownership tests passed, including 60–144 Hz cases.
- No DAPA admission/timing settings, gaze radii, controller layouts, CSX installation or upscaler settings are changed. The frame reset adds no GPU command or wait itself. Correct clearing and full-precision mask rendering have their normal GPU cost; no unchanged-game-FPS claim is made.

## Retest

Install the complete Hotfix 4 package and fully restart Skyrim through MO2. Both `root/openvr_api.dll` and `SKSE/Plugins/OpenCompositeInput.dll` are required; replacing only the runtime does not deliver the internal shader tags.

Confirm the runtime log identifies `4.3.7-custom-eye-test-hotfix4 / DAPA mask-frame-v1 + exact-mask-v1`, and the SKSE log identifies `4.3.7-custom-eye-test-hotfix4 / DAPA exact-mask-v1`.

For Lee, preserve Quest 3, VRS off, the same CSX and existing DAPA tuning. Check bare hands during movement, then the weapon equip/stow transition. If the fault returns, retain both OCU/SKSE logs from that run and record approximately when it started and whether equipping restored it. A DAPA-off comparison in the same place would distinguish DAPA-specific artifacts from the remaining rendering/streaming path. Do not infer successful pixel coverage from `maskDraws` alone; that counter records draws, not final depth matches.

All earlier Hotfix 3 terrain/cutout, ring overlay and runtime/controller corrections are retained. Their outstanding headset qualifications are unchanged.
